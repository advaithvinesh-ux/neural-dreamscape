✳01 / FIELD

Artificial Intelligence

How can computers perform tasks that usually need human intelligence?

Explore concept ＋
AI is the broad field of building systems that can reason, perceive, understand language, or assist with decisions. Examples include voice assistants and recommendation tools.
⌘02 / APPROACH

Machine Learning

What if a system could learn patterns from examples?

Explore concept ＋
Machine learning is a branch of AI in which algorithms learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.
⌬03 / TECHNIQUE

Deep Learning

How do layered neural networks find complex patterns?

Explore concept ＋
Deep learning uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns in images, sound, text, and other kinds of data.

✦ AI is the wider field; machine learning is one approach within AI; deep learning is one family of machine-learning methods.

AI IN THE REAL WORLD

From bold ideas to daily life.

AI already supports people in many fields. These examples show how learning from data can help solve practical problems.

✧01 / HEALTHCARE

Health & medicine

AI tools can help researchers study medical images, identify patterns, and support clinical decisions under professional supervision.

⌁02 / CLIMATE

Climate & nature

Models can analyze weather records, monitor forests, and help scientists understand environmental changes.
